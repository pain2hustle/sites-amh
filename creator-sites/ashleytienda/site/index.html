How do the reels play?

Press play to load the original Instagram player. These are the full original reels, not edited sequences. Playback depends on Instagram and may require opening the original link.

Where are saved reels kept?

Your watchlist is stored only in this browser. Tap the heart again to remove a reel, or clear the list from the watchlist window.

What does “More of me” open?

It opens an age-restricted platform after a fresh 18+ confirmation. Your answer is never stored or reused. The destination has its own access policies.

Does the collaboration form send a message?

No. It prepares a brief on your screen. You decide whether to copy it and send it through Instagram.
