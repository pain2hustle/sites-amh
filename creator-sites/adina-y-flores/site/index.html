A few details

Before you go.

What plays in the movie club?

Items marked Instagram are exact creator-posted reels discovered on the public profile. Their player loads only after you choose one; if sign-in or platform restrictions prevent playback, use the original-post link. Items marked Photo story are separate 15-second silent design previews made from supplied stills, not original social videos.

Where is my watchlist saved?

Only in this browser, on this device. No account is needed. Tap a story’s heart again to remove it.

Does the contact form send anything?

No. It prepares a local text draft for you to copy. You choose whether to contact the creator on the linked platform.
