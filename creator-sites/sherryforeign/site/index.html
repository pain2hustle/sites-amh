A few details

Before
you go.

How do the reels play?

Press play to load the original Instagram or YouTube player. The full videos are kept intact. Playback depends on the platform and may require opening the original link.

Where are saved reels kept?

Your watchlist is stored only in this browser. Tap the heart again to remove a reel, or clear the list from the watchlist window.

Are these original video frames?

The two Instagram covers are original public reel thumbnails. YouTube uses editorial title artwork because its thumbnail host was unavailable. Those graphics are not represented as video frames.

Does the collaboration form send a message?

No. It prepares a brief on your screen. You decide whether to copy it and send it using your own email app. The website never sends it for you.
